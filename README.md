# PolyBlur

[![Modrinth downloads](https://img.shields.io/modrinth/dt/xT45UG5o?style=flat&logo=modrinth&label=Modrinth&color=1bd96a)](https://modrinth.com/mod/polyblur)
[![CurseForge downloads](https://img.shields.io/curseforge/dt/1147793?style=flat&logo=curseforge&label=CurseForge&color=f16436)](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/polyblur)

PolyBlur adds motion blur to Minecraft, with three blur types to pick from.

- Phosphor blends each frame into the next for the classic trail effect
- Unity blurs along the direction your camera moves
- Hybrid uses Unity for the world and Phosphor for your held item (not on 1.21.5 yet)

Other things you can do:

- Set the blur strength for the world and for your hand separately
- Keep your held item sharp
- Switch between three Phosphor blending modes
- Raise or lower the Unity sample count to trade smoothness for FPS
- Have Unity blur while you walk too, instead of only when you turn the camera (not on 1.8.9)
- You can use `/polyblur` to open your settings

## Supported versions

- Minecraft 1.8.9 on Ornithe.
- Minecraft 1.21.1-26.3 on Fabric.

PolyBlur needs [OneConfig](https://modrinth.com/mod/oneconfig) and Fabric Language Kotlin.
